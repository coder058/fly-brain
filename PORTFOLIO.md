# Fly Lab — research testbed portfolio

This repository documents an independent research testbed. No entry is presented as a product, profitability result, living-fly simulation or biological claim.

## Current state

- **E0 / frozen object:** MaleCNS v1.0 is recorded as 165,122 neurons and 25,563,197 directed edges after the recorded filter. Source provenance and graph integrity are checked without rebuilding the protected graph.
- **EXP-INST-001 / limited instrument result:** a post-LIF feature-injection effect was detected under its registered setup. This is not a topology or memory result.
- **EXP-MEM-003 / `MEM_003_NO_PAIR`:** two registered maps wrote their cells, but no delay contained a live, rate-matched connectome/DP pair. No memory curve or paired contrast was produced.
- **Functional control repair:** four archived superclass-block controls had a local/global index error. The public erratum preserves those artifacts, and corrected runs plus audits are available. Their outcomes are exploratory after the repair, not independent confirmation or a memory result.
- **EXP-GLU-001 / `COMPLETE_METRIC_WITHHELD`:** all 12 seed checkpoints were written, but the preregistered aggregate polarity comparison was withheld because the validity gate was not met.
- **EXP-IGNITE-001 / `NO_COIGNITE`:** no primary connectome/DP pair was found under the registered operating points.
- **EXP-IGNITE-002 / `NO_COIGNITE`:** the authorized polarity sensitivity follow-up also found no primary pair. No further IGNITE ID is authorized.
- **N6 / `DEFERRED_HARDWARE`:** navigation work has documented prerequisites but has not run on hardware or a reproducible simulator.
- **N7 / `QUEUED_2VCPU`:** a future continual-learning or architecture smoke is documented but not executed.
- **N8 / bounded local queue:** the A2A prototype is loopback-only infrastructure with no external workers.

## Interpretation boundary

The closed E1 and EXP-006 hypothesis IDs may only be described as: “The tested implementation/configuration did not demonstrate a topology advantage under that experimental setup.” They must not be paraphrased as “biology has no value.”

Missing or invalid liveness rows are instrument outcomes, not scores against the connectome. A script that runs, or a measurable injected feature, does not by itself establish memory, topology advantage or biological intelligence.

## Public snapshot

The public repository contains code, documentation, tests and lightweight evidence artifacts. Raw MaleCNS files and the protected derived graph archive are intentionally excluded. See `README.md`, `RESEARCH_LEDGER.yaml` and `SCIENTIFIC_CLAIMS.md` for provenance and the complete evidence boundary.
